Fight together. Build your army. Outmatch your partner.

Necromancer × Magical Girl is a 3D local co-op action game for two players. Play as a Necromancer or a Magical Girl, explore a dungeon together, and turn defeated enemies into new recruits for your own army.

Help each other through the dungeon, master your character's abilities, and gather enough followers to take on your partner in the final clash!

⚔️ GAMEPLAY
Your goal: fight your way through the dungeon and build an army strong enough to outmatch your rival.
• Choose the Necromancer or the Magical Girl.
• Fight enemies with your character's attacks and abilities.
• Recruit defeated enemies to grow your own army.
• Lead your followers through the dungeon.
• Face your partner in the final clash!

🎮 LOCAL MULTIPLAYER
Two players. One computer. Split-screen co-op with a competitive twist.
Explore and fight together, while each of you builds a separate army. Coordinate your actions to progress, but keep an eye on the force your partner is gathering!
Select each player's input device on the character-selection screen. Gamepads are supported. This is local multiplayer; no online lobby or port forwarding is needed.

💾 HOW TO PLAY
1. Download and extract the entire Windows archive.
2. Open Necromancer × Magical Girl.exe inside the extracted folder.
3. Start a new game and select the input device for each player.
4. Choose your characters and enter the dungeon together.
Game language: menus and dialogue are in French.
